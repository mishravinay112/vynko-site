Record your screen with sound

Record a tab with its own sound, or your whole screen with sound from other apps like a WhatsApp call, and add your voice from the microphone, with live level bars so you can see sound is coming through.

Add to Chrome, it's free

How to do it

  1. Click the Vynko icon and open Record.
  2. Turn on Microphone to add your voice, and System sound for other apps. A level bar moves as sound comes in.
  3. Choose Tab to record this tab with its sound, or Window or screen, pick Entire screen and tick "Share system audio" in the sharing box.
  4. Press Stop. The video opens in its own tab, ready to trim and download as MP4 or WebM.

Good to know

  • Tab sound is recorded from the tab itself, without your speakers being picked up.
  • System sound works on Windows, ChromeOS and Mac (macOS 14.2 or newer with a recent Chrome). A single window has no sound of its own, so Vynko offers to add your computer's sound instead.
  • Click Microphone or System sound while recording to turn either on or off. With both on, use headphones so the call doesn't echo.
  • Recordings save to disk as you go, so a crash doesn't lose them.

Questions

Can Vynko record system audio?

Yes. Turn on System sound, choose Window or screen, pick Entire screen and tick Share system audio. It works on Windows, ChromeOS and Mac (macOS 14.2 or newer with a recent Chrome).

Will my voice and the tab sound be mixed?

Yes, both go into one soundtrack.

Is there a time limit?

No. You can set an optional auto-stop if you want one.

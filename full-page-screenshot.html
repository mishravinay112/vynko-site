Full page screenshot of any website

Vynko scrolls the page for you, waits for lazy-loaded images, hides sticky headers so they don't repeat, and stitches everything into one long image.

Add to Chrome, it's free

How to do it

  1. Click the Vynko icon and choose Full page, or press Alt+Shift+F.
  2. Wait a moment while it scrolls. Progress shows in the popup and on the icon.
  3. Edit, then download as PNG, JPG or WebP, or copy it.

Good to know

  • Works on apps that scroll an inner panel, like Gmail and Slack.
  • Very long pages are cut at the browser's maximum image height (about 32,000 pixels).
  • Emails and phone numbers on the page can be blurred in one click.

Questions

Why do some sticky headers disappear?

Vynko hides fixed and sticky elements after the first screen so they don't repeat down the image.

Does it load lazy images?

Yes. It scrolls through the page first so images and lists load before capturing.

Is there a page length limit?

Only the browser's maximum image size, about 32,000 pixels tall.

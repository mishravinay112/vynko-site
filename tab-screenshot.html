Screenshot a Chrome tab in one click

Capture exactly what you see in the current tab, without the browser bars, then mark it up, blur private details and save or copy it.

Add to Chrome, it's free

How to do it

  1. Add Vynko to Chrome or Edge and pin it to the toolbar.
  2. Open the tab, click the Vynko icon and choose Visible, or press Alt+Shift+V.
  3. The screenshot opens in the editor. Draw arrows, add text, blur emails and phone numbers, crop, then press Ctrl/Cmd+S or Copy.

Good to know

  • Only the page content is captured, never the address bar or other tabs.
  • Set a 3, 5 or 10 second timer to open a menu or hover state first.
  • Choose 100% for full sharpness or 50% for a quarter of the file size.

Questions

Does Vynko capture the address bar?

No. Visible captures only the page area of the current tab.

Can I copy the screenshot instead of saving it?

Yes. Press Copy in the editor and paste it into Slack, email or a document.

Is it free?

Yes. No watermark, no account, no limits.

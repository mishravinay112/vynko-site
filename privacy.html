Privacy Policy

Summary

Vynko doesn't collect, store, sell or share any personal data. There are no accounts, servers, analytics, ads, cookies or tracking.

What stays on your device

  • Your settings, such as formats, theme and editor colors (browser extension storage).
  • Recordings while they're in progress, kept in the browser's local database so they can be recovered after a crash. They're removed once a new recording starts after the old one was saved.
  • Screenshots while the editor is open, and the files you choose to download.

All of this stays in your browser profile. Uninstalling Vynko removes it, apart from files you downloaded.

Permissions

  • activeTab, scripting: work only on the tab you choose, to capture it, show the area picker and the on-page editor.
  • tabCapture, offscreen: record the tab when you start a recording.
  • downloads: save your files.
  • storage: remember your settings.
  • desktopCapture: open your browser's own "choose what to share" box when you pick Choose, so you can record any tab, a window or your screen. Only what you pick there is recorded.

Network

Vynko makes no network requests. Fonts and icons are built in. The extension itself never connects to a server. Three links open a web page in a new tab: the PayPal link and the review link, only if you click them, and a short optional feedback form on Google Forms that your browser opens when you uninstall Vynko. The form address includes the Vynko version number and nothing else. PayPal's and Google's own privacy policies apply on those pages.

Your rights

Privacy laws such as the GDPR and UK GDPR in Europe and the CCPA in California give you rights over data a company holds about you. I don't hold any data about you, so there's nothing to access, correct, delete or sell. To clear Vynko's local data, uninstall it or clear its extension data in your browser.

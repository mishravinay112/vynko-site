Screenshot your entire screen

Capture everything on a monitor: the desktop, every window and the taskbar, then crop or annotate it in the editor.

Add to Chrome, it's free

How to do it

  1. Click the Vynko icon and choose Window or screen.
  2. In the sharing box, open the Entire screen tab and pick a monitor.
  3. Edit the screenshot and save or copy it.

Good to know

  • With several monitors, pick the one you want; each is its own option.
  • Use Crop in the editor to keep just part of the screen.
  • A timer gives you time to set the screen up first.

Questions

Does it work with two monitors?

Yes. Your browser lists each screen separately.

Can I crop it afterwards?

Yes, with the Crop tool in the editor. Undo brings the full image back.

Is it free?

Yes, with no watermark and no account.

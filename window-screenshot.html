Screenshot any window or app

Not just web pages: capture another browser window, a desktop app or a single tab you are not looking at, using your browser's own share picker.

Add to Chrome, it's free

How to do it

  1. Click the Vynko icon, then in Anywhere on your screen choose Window or screen.
  2. Pick the window in your browser's sharing box and press Share.
  3. The screenshot opens in the editor, ready to mark up and save.

Good to know

  • The list of windows comes from your browser and operating system.
  • On macOS, Chrome needs Screen Recording permission in System Settings the first time.
  • Nothing is shared or uploaded: the frame is captured on your device.

Questions

Can I screenshot an app that isn't a browser?

Yes, any window your system lets Chrome share, such as Word, Excel, Figma or Terminal.

Why does macOS ask for permission?

macOS requires Screen Recording permission for any app that captures windows, including Chrome.

Is the picture uploaded anywhere?

No. It stays on your device.

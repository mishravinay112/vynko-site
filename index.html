How to use Vynko

How to take a full page screenshot in Chrome

  1. Add Vynko from the Chrome Web Store and pin it to the toolbar.
  2. Open the page, click the Vynko icon and choose Full page, or press Alt+Shift+F.
  3. Vynko scrolls the page, hides sticky headers so they don't repeat, and opens the result in the editor. Click Download or Copy.

How to record your screen in Chrome with audio

  1. Click the Vynko icon and open Record.
  2. Choose Tab for this tab with its sound, Area for part of it, or Choose for any window or your whole screen.
  3. Turn on Microphone to add your voice and Webcam to add a camera bubble in the corner.
  4. Click the icon again and press Stop. The video opens in the editor, ready to trim and download as WebM or MP4.

How to record your screen as a GIF

In Record, set the format to GIF and record as usual. Or record a video, then in the editor click GIF and pick small (480 px), medium (640 px) or large (960 px). Each size shows an estimated file size before you save.

How to trim or cut a screen recording

In the editor, move the playhead and press S to split. Click any part on the timeline to remove it, and click again to bring it back. Download saves only the parts you kept.

How to screenshot another window or your whole screen

In Screenshot, choose Window, then pick the window or screen in your browser's sharing box. The image opens in the editor.

How to blur emails and phone numbers in a screenshot

Take a screenshot. If the page shows emails or phone numbers, the editor shows Blur private info. One click covers them all. You can also drag the Blur tool over anything else, such as names, keys or card numbers.

Private by design

I don't run any servers for Vynko, so there is nowhere for your data to go. The extension has no analytics, ads or tracking, and loads no code from the internet. It requests no host permissions, and its content security policy only allows connections to local blob and data URLs. Your webcam and microphone go only into the recording you save.

Check it yourself: right-click the Vynko popup, choose Inspect, open the Network tab, then take a screenshot and record a clip. The Network tab stays empty.

Questions

Is Vynko really free, with no watermark?

Yes. No ads, no watermark, no time limit, no paid tier. If it saves you time, there's an optional donate link.

Does Vynko upload my recordings or screenshots?

No. Captures are created in your browser and saved to your device.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no sign-up and no login.

Can it record system audio?

Tab recordings include the tab's sound. When you pick a window or your screen with Choose, the browser's sharing box offers to include system audio on Windows and ChromeOS. On a Mac, only tab sound and your microphone can be recorded.

Can I record my webcam with my screen?

Yes. Turn on Webcam in the Record tab. A round camera bubble appears in the corner of the video.

Where are my files saved?

In your Downloads folder, or a folder inside it that you choose in Settings.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Everything it needs is inside the extension.

Does it work in Microsoft Edge?

Yes. Vynko works in Chrome, Edge and other Chromium browsers.

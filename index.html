Vynko: a free screen recorder and screenshot tool that never uploads anything

Vynko is a Chrome and Edge extension for recording your screen and taking screenshots. It has no servers, no accounts, no ads and no watermark, and it makes no network requests. What you capture stays on your device.

Add Vynko from the Chrome Web Store

Free. Works in Chrome and Microsoft Edge.

What it does

Screenshots: capture the visible area, the full scrolling page or a selected area. Save as PNG, JPG or WebP, copy to the clipboard, or drag the image into another app.

Screen recording: record the current tab, part of a tab, any tab, a window or the full screen. You can pause and resume, add microphone audio, highlight clicks, and export as WebM, MP4 or GIF at 720p, 1080p or 4K. An optional time limit and crash recovery are included.

Editing: draw arrows, boxes, circles, lines and text, use a highlighter, add numbered steps and stickers, blur sensitive areas, crop, and undo. Keyboard shortcuts, light and dark themes and screen reader support are built in.

Why it is private

The developer, Vinay Mishra, does not run any servers for Vynko, so there is nowhere for your data to go. The extension contains no analytics, no ads, no tracking and no code loaded from the internet. Fonts and icons ship inside the extension. Its manifest requests no host permissions, and a content security policy blocks any connection except to local blob and data URLs.

Three links open a web page in a new tab: the donate link and the review link, only if you click them, and an optional feedback form on Google Forms that your browser opens when you uninstall. The extension itself never connects to anything.

Check it yourself

  1. Open the extension's popup, right-click it and choose Inspect, then open the Network tab.
  2. Take a screenshot and record a short clip.
  3. The Network tab stays empty. You can repeat this on chrome://extensions by inspecting the service worker.

The permissions list on the Chrome Web Store page shows what the extension can access: activeTab, scripting, tabCapture, offscreen, downloads, storage and desktopCapture.

Questions

Does Vynko upload my recordings or screenshots?

No. Captures are created in your browser and saved to your device. There is no server to receive them.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no sign-up and no login.

Is it really free?

Yes. There are no ads, no watermark and no paid tier. If it saves you time, there is a donate link in the extension and on the help page.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Everything it needs is bundled in the extension.

Where are my files saved?

Wherever your browser saves downloads. While you edit or record, the data is held in your browser's local storage so a crash does not lose it, and it is cleared when a new recording starts or when you uninstall.

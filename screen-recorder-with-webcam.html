Record your screen with your webcam

Add a round webcam bubble to tab, window or screen recordings, with your voice from the microphone. The webcam is its own layer, so after recording you can move it, resize it or take it out. Good for tutorials, bug reports and walkthroughs.

Add to Chrome, it's free

How to do it

  1. Click the Vynko icon, open Record and turn on Webcam and Microphone. Your browser asks once for each.
  2. Pick the corner and size the webcam starts at: bottom left, bottom right, top left or top right, small, medium or large.
  3. Record a tab, part of a tab, a window or your whole screen.
  4. Stop to open the video in the editor. Click the webcam to drag it anywhere, resize it from its corner, or delete it. Then download.

Good to know

  • The camera and microphone go only into the video you save, on your device.
  • The bubble is round and mirrored, like looking in a mirror.
  • Save original downloads the screen recording and the webcam as two separate files, whatever you edited.
  • If you turn off editing before saving, or record a GIF, the webcam is drawn into the video at the corner you picked.

Questions

Do I need an account?

No. There is no sign-up and nothing is uploaded.

Can I move or remove the webcam after recording?

Yes. It's its own layer: drag it anywhere, drag its corner to resize it, or delete it in the editor.

Which formats can I save?

MP4, WebM or GIF, and you can cut and edit the video first.
